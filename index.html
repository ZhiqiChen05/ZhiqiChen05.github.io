Zhiqi Chen

Understanding how models acquire new capabilities and use what they already know.

I study how models gain and use capabilities, and develop training-data strategies and reinforcement learning methods that expand reasoning and generalization in large language models. I also use interactive learning for agents, including robots, to probe and extend what models can do.

I am a B.Eng. student in Automation at Tsinghua University and a member of the THU Tong Class (AGI Class), graduating in 2027. I work with Gao Huang at LeapLab and with Andrea Zanette and Haiwen Feng at Impossible Inc. My co-first-authored study of reinforcement learning for LLM reasoning received the NeurIPS 2025 Best Paper Runner-Up Award.

02Interactive agents

Research blog · 2026 Project lead

Code as Primitives: Harnessing Frontier Models to Synthesize Low-Level Robotic Skills

Zhiqi Chen*, Djengo Cyun-Jyun Fang*, Guanning Zeng, Xiuyu Li, Haiwen Feng, Andrea Zanette

We use frontier language models to synthesize robotic controllers and refine them through simulation feedback, without demonstrations. The language model stays frozen, and the resulting controllers reach over 90% held-out success across 75 DexVerse tasks and 99.2% across four Adroit tasks, with no model calls during execution.

My contribution. I led problem formulation, method design, implementation, and evaluation. I proposed separating controller-program revision from numerical parameter search to give the agent focused feedback for improving its design.
